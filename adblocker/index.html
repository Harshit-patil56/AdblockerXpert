Frequently asked questions.

An adblocker is a software application or browser extension designed to prevent online advertisements from being displayed on websites.

Ad blockers will stop ads in their tracks so that you can enjoy web pages faster, reduce data usage and save your device's battery life.

In most regions, using adblockers is legal. Adblockers operate by blocking or filtering content from websites, but they don't typically violate any laws. Users have the right to control the content that appears on their devices, and adblockers are generally considered a tool that empowers users to customize their online experience.

Websites need money to stay free. Support them by allowing Acceptable Ads (enabled by default). Want to hide all ads? No problem.

Adblockers are programmed to block specific content from displaying; they cannot take any action beyond that. Therefore, they should not be considered a potential security concern, as they are unable to access any of your personal information.

If you are experiencing issues with a specific website while using an adblocker, you can try the following steps to resolve the problem.

  • Disable Adblocker
  • Check Adblocker Settings
  • Update Adblocker
  • Clear Browser Cache
  • Check Browser Extensions
  • Contact Website Support
